Privacy policy

Information about this website and the details you share when arranging laundry services.

Draft for business review

This page is available in the local preview. Business policy details must be confirmed before publication.

Who to contact

Dr Wash Laundry is located at UCC Ayensu, next to BBF Barbering Shop. For questions about your information or to make a privacy request, call 0246150683, message us on WhatsApp or visit during opening hours.

Information used to arrange an order

When you contact us, you may share your name, phone number, service requirements, delivery or pickup address, messages and payment reference. Share only the information needed to arrange your service. Do not send a Mobile Money PIN, password or one-time security code.

Why information is needed

Order information is used to respond to your enquiry, confirm service details, arrange pickup or delivery, communicate about payment and resolve service concerns. Information should only be shared with people or providers who need it for those purposes or where required by law.

Website cookies and tracking

This version of the website has no account registration, advertising trackers, analytics scripts, contact form or online payment collection. Its code does not set cookies or use browser storage. Images, fonts and animation files are served with the website. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses, browser details and access logs.

Phone, WhatsApp and Mobile Money

Following a contact link opens your phone service or WhatsApp. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies. Mobile Money providers also process payment information under their own terms. This website does not receive your Mobile Money credentials.

Keeping information

Order and communication records may need to be retained to provide the service, resolve concerns or meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. The business must confirm its retention schedule and operational handling before this policy is published.

Your choices and privacy requests

Contact Dr Wash Laundry to ask about personal information held about you, request correction, or request deletion or restriction where applicable. We may need to confirm your identity to handle a request. You may also raise a concern with Ghana’s Data Protection Commission.

Privacy rights information: Ghana Data Protection Commission.
