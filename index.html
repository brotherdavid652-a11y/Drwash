Your questions,

Let’s talk on WhatsApp

What does the GH₵100 regular price cover?+

Everyday Fresh includes washing, drying, ironing, and folding. GH₵100 is the minimum charge, not a fixed price for a specified quantity. Tell us about your laundry so we can confirm your final price.

How soon will my laundry be ready?+

We’ll confirm the completion time when we discuss your order. Need it sooner? Express service is available; contact us for pricing and timing.

Can you collect and deliver my laundry?+

Yes, we offer pickup and delivery. Share your location when you call or message us on WhatsApp. We’ll confirm coverage, fees, and scheduling before you go ahead.

How do I pay?+

We accept cash and Mobile Money. Confirm when to pay and the Mobile Money payment details directly with Dr Wash Laundry when arranging your order.

Do you clean curtains and special fabrics?+

Yes. We clean curtains, drapery, and window coverings, and offer special care for delicate fabrics. Contact us to discuss your items and confirm pricing.
