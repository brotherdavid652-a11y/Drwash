- **Phone:** 0246150683
- **WhatsApp:** [Chat with Dr Wash Laundry](https://wa.me/message/JEGEROEOTPZ5K1)

## 2. Services

A dedicated **Services** section will list:

- Washing, ironing, and folding
- Quilt cleaning

- Sneaker care

A separate **Packages & Pricing** section will feature large cards with clear descriptions and prices.

Prices for drapery, window-cover cleaning, and special fabric care remain to be decided.

## 4. Design & Appearance

The website will use a **gold-and-black theme** with a clean, professional layout.

The design will prioritize phones, with readable text, large package cards, simple navigation, and prominent **“Call Now”** and **“Order on WhatsApp”** buttons.

## 5. Website Sections

The proposed first version will be a single-page website containing:

1. **Welcome:** Business name, headline, a short introduction, and order buttons.
2. **Services:** The full list of cleaning and laundry services.
3. **Packages & Pricing:** Package cards and express-service details.
4. **How to Order:** Simple instructions for requesting and confirming a service.
5. **Pickup & Delivery:** Information about collection and delivery arrangements.
6. **Contact & Opening Hours:** Location, business hours, contact links, and payment methods.
